Profile & Philosophy

About Me

Bridging software engineering, explainable artificial intelligence research, and purposeful leadership to solve complex real-world challenges.

I am a Software Engineering graduate holding a BSc (Hons) in Computer Software Engineering from the University of Plymouth, currently expanding my academic horizon by pursuing an MSc in Computer Science (Business Computing) with the University of Staffordshire.

My passion resides at the intersection of artificial intelligence, emerging technologies, and business innovation. In my academic research, I focus on trustworthy clinical AI, having formulated a quantitative framework for human-in-the-loop explainable AI (XAI) and presented my findings at an Elsevier-associated international conference in Wuhan, China.

Beyond academia, I serve as a Business Analyst Intern at Ceylinco Life Insurance Limited, bridging computational methodologies with enterprise workflows. As the Secretary and former Director of Professional Development of the Rotaract Club of Colombo Metropolitan, I have led national-scale projects, chaired large creative productions, and actively mentored youth. I believe true technological progress thrives when analytical rigor is matched with empathetic leadership and social responsibility.
